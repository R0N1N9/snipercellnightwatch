How to play

You and your friend start on opposite ridgelines, ~800 m apart. One life per round. First to 4 rounds wins.

  • Bullets drop and drift. Set your zero (Q/E) to the range, read the wind from flags, smoke and drifting embers, and lead moving targets.
  • Scope glint gives you away when you're scoped in and pointed at them. 12× glints most, 4× least.
  • Listen. A near miss gives you a sharp crack, then the rifle's boom follows. The longer the gap, the further away they are (sound moves ~340 m/s).
  • Uplinks A·B·C (town, dam, ski lift): stand in the ring for 8 s. Capturing one pings your enemy's position 3 times. If time runs out, most uplinks wins.
  • Trees hide you but don't stop bullets. Rocks, walls and buildings do.

How to play
  • Objectives: breach the fence → plant a relay in the Electrical Room → drop the Site Chief and take his badge → badge in at the vault while the spotter cracks it → hold the console through the upload (the spotter fights SENTINEL’s trace) → pull the drive (lockdown!) → out the loading dock to the LZ.
  • Stealth: guards fill a ? then ! meter when they see you. Dark, prone, still and cloaked are all harder to see. An alert guard radios the alarm 1.5 s later — drop him first. Bodies get found. Unsuppressed shots are heard 70 m away.
  • SENTINEL sends fresh patrols out of the barracks and the south gate, and relaunches patrol drones. Shoot the drones down.
  • Sentinel troops: Officers are small and unshielded — alone and shot at, they shriek and run. Juggernauts wear a recharging energy shell; break it and they charge with an energy blade. Bulwarks hide behind a hard-light wall — shoot through its sight-port, catch them lowering it to fire, or flank. Tower bulwarks have beam rifles: watch for the red laser. Headshots kill anything, with any weapon.
  • Shooter: F active camo, H deadeye, X sticky grenade (sticks to enemies, then blows), Z round type, T sync mark, Q ping / mark, G use your IFAK (full health), 1/2/3 rifle/sidearm/knife, B suppressor, N night vision, TAB map.
  • Pings & requests: tap V (or PING) to ping what you're looking at; hold it for the wheel — LOOK, ENEMY, MOVE, HOLD, ON ME, COVER, plus SUPPLY, SCAN, EMP, DECOY, TAKE OUT THE LIGHTS, DRONE requests. The spotter approves with Y / SEND. Right-click or long-press the tablet map for the same wheel.
  • Exfil: the van east of the fence. Spotter takes the driver's seat, shooter the passenger seat (hold E at the door).
  • Spotter: TAB tablet (click contacts to tag, cameras/doors to hack, 1–9 payloads, C hold crack, X hold fight trace, right-click ping), F recon drone, Q ping / mark, T sync mark, G use your IFAK (full health), B suppressor, N night vision.
  • Both: E interact / silent takedown from behind / revive. A downed operator bleeds out in 16 s.
